# The grand show

Every final project, presented live, to the whole studio and any guests

---

## Every slot is fixed

Every student presents in a fixed slot, and the schedule doesn't stretch —
know what you're showing before your slot starts. The work runs live too,
not a recording of it working once, so you can name the one decision
you're least sure about rather than hiding it.

---

## How you're marked

The holistic mark is formed the way the [final project
brief](/assessments/final-project/) describes — worth rereading before
your slot.

---

## Once you're done presenting

The show stays up for the rest of the day — bring people through once your
own slot is done.

---

## Watch someone else's track

Watch a track you didn't work in. The round trip looks different from the
other pillar's side.
