# The grand show

Every final project, presented live, to the whole studio and any guests

---

## How the crit runs

- every student presents in a fixed slot — the schedule doesn't stretch,
  so know what you're showing before your slot starts
- the work runs live, not a recording of it working once — and you can
  name the one decision you're least sure about rather than hiding it
- the holistic mark is formed the way the [final project
  brief](/assessments/final-project/) describes — worth rereading before
  your slot

---

## After your slot

- the show stays up for the rest of the day — bring people through once
  your own slot is done
- watch a track you didn't work in; the round trip looks different from
  the other pillar's side
