# Tracking

A camera feed, watched live, rather than a single photo taken once

---

## Recap

- a tracking pipeline captures continuous position and motion, not one
  static shape the way weeks 1–2's scanning did
- the round-trip pillar turns its final corner: physical media, but live
  rather than captured once
- tracked input is designed to feed straight into the state machines and
  rule systems from weeks 7–8
- today's crit is Final Planning & Ideation — pitching the final's theme
  and technique combination

---

## Turning a camera feed into a usable signal, step by step

1. Start from a live feed rather than a single photo — every frame is a
   new capture, arriving continuously instead of once.
2. Isolate what you actually want to track from the background: simple
   motion or frame-differencing to find what changed between two frames,
   or colour/blob tracking to find a particular hue or shape.
3. Reduce that isolated region to a small continuous number stream — an
   x/y position, a size, a count — the same way a sensor reading from
   week 4 was a number your code could use.
4. Smooth or threshold that stream so noise between frames doesn't read
   as a state change on its own.
5. Feed the resulting stream into a state machine or rule system from
   weeks 7–8 exactly as if it were a sensor: transitions triggered by
   what the camera now sees, not what a button reports.
