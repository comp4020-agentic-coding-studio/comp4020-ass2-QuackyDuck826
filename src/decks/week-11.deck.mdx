## Come with a specific problem

Bring your final project plan from week 9's crit, and use the session for
one-on-one time with whichever tutor fits what you're stuck on. There's no
set schedule beyond the two tracks — come with the specific thing you need
help with, not a general question.

gap, that's this week's problem to close, not next week's.
