The grand show. Every final project is presented to an audience, not just
graded in private — the closing move for a studio course, and the point
where each student's own version of the round trip becomes visible to
everyone else's.

## Outline

- **final crit** — every student presents, in a fixed slot, to the whole
  studio and any guests
- what "presenting" means here: the work runs live, and you can name the
  decision you're least sure about
- how the holistic mark is formed — see the [final project
  brief](/assessments/final-project/) for the marking description
- the show stays up for the rest of the day — bring people through
