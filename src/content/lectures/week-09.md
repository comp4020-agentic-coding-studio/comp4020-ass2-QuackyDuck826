The round-trip pillar begins its final turn: camera-based tracking is a
richer, continuous form of the digitizing work from weeks 1–2 — instead of
capturing a static object once, a camera feed captures motion, presence or
position live, ready to feed into the interactive systems from Programming
II.

## Outline

- what a tracking pipeline captures that a single scan doesn't: continuous
  position and motion, not one static shape
- combining a tracked input with a state machine or rule system from weeks
  7–8
- **Final Planning & Ideation crit** — propose your final's theme and which
  three-plus techniques it combines, with rationale
