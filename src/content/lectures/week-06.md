The last week before the break connects the two pillars taught so far: serial
communication carries a sensor's readings off the microcontroller and into
the creative-coding environment from week 3, or into the digitizing pipeline
from weeks 1–2. This is the technical hinge Studio Brief 2 and everything
after it depends on.

## Outline

- serial data over USB: what a baud rate is, and what can go wrong
- reading a live sensor stream into a Programming I sketch
- putting the whole loop together: physical input, through the board, into
  code, back out to an effect
- over the break: nothing due, but Studio Brief 2 (due week 7, first week
  back) draws on everything from weeks 1–6
