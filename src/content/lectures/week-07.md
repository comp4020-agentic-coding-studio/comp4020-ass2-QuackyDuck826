Welcome back. With a shared base now in place across both audiences —
digitizing, creative coding, and physical computing — Programming II goes
deeper: state machines as a way of structuring anything that behaves
differently depending on what happened before, and input handling that
doesn't fall apart when two things happen at once.

## Outline

- why "the program is in one of a few states" is a useful way to think about
  interactive work, physical or on-screen
- input handling: debouncing, edge-detection, and the bugs that show up
  when you don't do either
- **Studio Brief 2 crit** — demos combining weeks 1–6, feedback on what to
  carry into the final
- small game engines and rule systems next week — building on the state
  machine as a foundation
