No new material this week. This is deliberately a studio week with two
parallel tracks running at once, not one lecture: a "code clinic" for
students who want more time with programming or the interactive-systems
pillar, and a "make clinic" for students who want more time with digitizing,
physical computing or fabrication. Move between them, or stay in one — this
is time to close whichever gap the semester exposed before the final.

## Outline

- **code clinic** (Idris) — programming and interactive-systems help, running
  continuously through the session
- **make clinic** (Marisol) — digitizing, physical-computing and fabrication
  help, running continuously through the session
- one-on-one time on your final project plan from week 9's crit
- no set schedule beyond the two tracks — come with what you need help with
