The physical-computing pillar opens with input: a microcontroller reading
light, distance, motion or force from the physical world and turning it into
a number your code can use. This is also the week Studio Brief 1 is due — it
asks you to combine a digitizing technique with the Programming I on-ramp,
so today's crit looks at how that combination actually held together.

## Outline

- microcontroller basics: what "reading a sensor" means as a loop
- a walkthrough of two or three common sensor types and where each is a good
  fit
- **Studio Brief 1 crit** — short demos, feedback on the digitizing +
  Programming I combination
- actuators next week: what changes when the microcontroller writes back out
  to the physical world instead of just reading it
