Marisol trained in expanded sculpture and spent a decade making work that
moved — kinetic pieces, motorised installations, a few pieces of furniture
that were not supposed to walk but did. Code entered the practice as a tool
for driving motors, not as a subject in its own right, and that is the
crossing *Round Trip* is built to shorten for the next student who has to
make it.

She teaches the opening digitizing weeks and the complex-physical-media unit
at the end of semester, and convenes the final crit.

Office is in the Invented Disciplines building, third floor. Studio hours are
posted on the sessions page each week; email is for anything that cannot wait
until the next one. If a question is about a specific brief rather than the
course as a whole, ask the tutor running that week's session first — Marisol
picks up anything that needs the convenor's call.
