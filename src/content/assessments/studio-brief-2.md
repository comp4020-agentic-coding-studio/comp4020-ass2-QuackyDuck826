## The brief

> Get a signal from the physical world, through a microcontroller, into a
> program, and let the program change something in the physical world back.

Combine at least two techniques from weeks 1–6: a digitizing technique, the
Programming I on-ramp, and the sensor/actuator/serial work from the
physical-computing pillar. The bridging week's serial-comms material is what
makes this brief different from Studio Brief 1 — the loop should actually
close, sensor to code to physical effect, not stop halfway.

## What you submit

A video under two minutes showing the loop running end to end, plus the
working files (firmware, sketch, and wiring photo or diagram). Name the
techniques combined and where each shows up in the video.
