## The brief

> Show up, and do the work in front of you that week.

Every tutorial session (see [Tutorials](/sessions/)) has a hands-on
component and something you leave with — a scanned model, a mapped
projection, a working sensor loop, an extended rule system, a fabricated
part. This mark tracks whether that happened for you, week to week, across
the whole semester, rather than asking you to submit anything separately.

Coming to tutorial time is also where you get your first real look at each
technique, well before it's due anywhere: a rough, guided first attempt at
scanning, wiring a sensor, or extending a rule system, with a tutor next to
you when it goes wrong. That's the starting point Studio Briefs 1 and 2 and
the final project then ask you to build on — turning up isn't just what's
marked here, it's what makes the graded work further down the semester
possible.

## What's checked

Nothing is submitted for this one. A tutor notes engagement during tutorial
time itself — whether you're there, working on that week's activity, and
able to show what you made when asked. It runs alongside the two studio
briefs and the final project rather than duplicating them: those are marked
on the work; this is marked on whether the studio time behind that work
actually happened.
