## The brief

> Take something physical, get it into the machine, and make the machine do
> something with it that wasn't possible before it was digitized.

Combine one technique from Digitizing Media I (scanning, photogrammetry or
projection mapping) with one idea from Programming I: Creative Coding
Foundations. The combination is the point — a scan sitting next to a sketch
you made separately doesn't answer the brief; a scan that *drives* the sketch,
or a sketch that changes how the scan is displayed, does.

This is the first place your studio pairing shows up in submitted work. If
your pair leaned on each other across the art/code gap, that's expected —
name who did what.

## What you submit

A video under two minutes showing the piece running, plus the working files
(sketch, scan or capture data, and anything else needed to reproduce it). The
video is what gets shown at the crit; the files are what a marker can check
against it.
