## The brief

> Before you build the final project, write down what it is, what it's made
> of, and what could go wrong.

This is deliberately small — five percent of the course — because its job is
to catch a plan that's too big, too vague, or missing a technique, while
there's still time to fix it. It is not marked on how good the idea sounds; it
is marked on whether the plan is specific enough that someone else could tell,
from reading it, whether the finished project answered it.

## What you submit

One page: the theme in a sentence, the three-plus techniques and why each is
there, and the single risk you're least sure you can solve.
