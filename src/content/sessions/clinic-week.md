## Before the session

Bring your final project plan from week 9 and whatever's furthest behind —
code, or a physical build.

## In the session

1. **Decide which clinic track you need first**, based on your final project
   plan from week 9 and whatever's furthest behind on it. The honest version
   of this question isn't "which half do I enjoy" but "which half of my plan
   would fail if I tried to demo it today" — most final projects fail by
   neglecting the half of the toolkit their author was never confident in,
   not the half they chose to lead with.
2. **Go to the code clinic or the make clinic — there's no single
   schedule.** Both tracks run at the same time, in different parts of the
   studio, for the whole session, because a wiring fault and a
   state-machine bug don't share a bottleneck: putting them on one shared
   schedule would mean queuing behind a tutor mid-explanation of the other
   half's material. Instead each track runs continuously with a tutor
   dedicated to it:
   - **Code clinic** (Idris) covers serial comms, state machines and rule
     systems, and the creative-coding side of your pipeline.
   - **Make clinic** (Marisol) covers wiring faults, sensor and actuator
     problems, scanning and projection setup, and fabrication file prep.
3. **Work on whichever half of the project you brought**, with a tutor from
   that track on hand. A good session here ends with the specific fault
   fixed or the specific pattern understood well enough that you could
   reproduce it yourself afterward — not just watched a tutor fix it while
   you held the board.
4. **Move to the other clinic at any point if your final project needs
   both.** Most final projects do, since the plan from week 9 already
   commits you to three-plus techniques spanning both halves of the loop.
   The clinic student to avoid becoming is the one who parks in whichever
   track feels comfortable for the whole session and never crosses over —
   they leave clinic week having reinforced their strong half and arrive at
   the final crit with one entire half of the toolkit still untouched.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. It exists so nobody arrives at the final crit
having spent the whole semester avoiding their weaker half.
