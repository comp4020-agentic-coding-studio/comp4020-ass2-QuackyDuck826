## Before the session

Bring the final project, running, set up before your slot starts — changeover
time between presenters is short.

## In the session

1. **Set up your final project before your slot starts**: laptop charged and
   plugged in, board powered on and already running its code, camera or
   projector aimed and focused. Do this before your slot, not during it —
   changeover time between presenters is short because the whole studio and
   any guests are seated through a fixed run of slots, and a presenter still
   plugging in cables eats into the next person's time as well as their own.
   If your setup depends on room lighting or projector throw distance, check
   it in the room itself beforehand; a focus that looked right in the
   corridor often isn't right under the crit's actual lights.
2. **Present in your fixed slot, in front of the whole studio and any
   guests.** The mark is holistic against your own plan from Final Planning
   & Ideation, so what the audience needs to see is the piece running and
   standing on its own — not you narrating what it would do if a sensor
   were reading correctly. Guests in particular have no context beyond
   what's in front of them, so a presentation that leans on spoken
   explanation instead of the working piece is the clearest sign something
   isn't actually finished.
3. **Name the three-plus techniques your project combines and the decision
   you are least sure about.** Naming the techniques is how the marker
   checks they're load-bearing rather than decorative — the same test the
   final-project brief itself sets. Naming the one decision you're least
   sure about is the harder, more useful move: it's an admission of a
   specific risk rather than a request for general praise, and it's usually
   the single most useful thing you say in your slot.
4. **Leave the project running as part of the show**, which stays up for
   the rest of the day once every slot is done. This is the last session of
   the course, and the show is where the semester's work is seen by people
   beyond the marker — unplugging the moment your slot ends trades that for
   a few minutes back that you don't actually need.

## Afterwards

The final project is marked holistically against the brief — see the
[assessment page](/assessments/final-project/). This is also the last session
of the course.
