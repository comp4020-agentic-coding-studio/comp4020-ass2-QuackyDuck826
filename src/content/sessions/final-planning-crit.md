## Before the session

Bring a one-page plan: the theme, the three-plus techniques, and the biggest
risk as you see it.

## In the session

1. Deliver a short pitch of your one-page plan: the theme, the three-plus
   techniques, and the biggest risk.
2. Take feedback focused on scope and risk rather than execution — nothing
   is built yet.
3. Check the feedback against your plan for hours it quietly assumes but the
   remaining weeks don't have, before you've spent any of them.

## Afterwards

The plan itself is marked holistically — see the [assessment
page](/assessments/final-planning/) — and it's what the final crit in week 12
compares your finished project against.
